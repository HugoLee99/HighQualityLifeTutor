## Interpretive conclusions used by this skill

1. “Sun studies cognition, Alan studies the body, yellow hair studies mentality” is a useful public shorthand, but too shallow for coaching. The operational distinction here is map/optionality, capability/maintenance, and desire/contact.
2. Thin muscle began as a body and aesthetic idea. Extending it to work, learning, or systems is an explicit project inference, not a claim that Alan published a complete cross-domain theory.
3. Yellow hair is treated as an archetype. It is not a real third expert and should not be described as one.
4. Persona-style lines in outputs are generated approximations, not verbatim quotations.
5. Health, investment, relationship, and AI claims from the source material require independent judgment. Popularity and confidence are not evidence.

## Update rule

When new first-party material materially changes a lens, update the model and record the source. Do not chase every meme variation. Prefer repeated behavior and long-form explanations over a viral caption.
